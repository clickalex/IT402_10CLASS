Answer Key — All 140 (with one-line Why)

QAnswerWhy (one line)
1ii. Communicator and audienceevery communication needs a sender (communicator) who sends and a receiver (audience) who receives — these two ends are its major components.
2ii. Non-verbal communicationgestures, expressions and tone need no language or literacy — even babies use them, so it is the most basic/simplest form.
3iv. Both i. and iii.posters/banners (visual) + announcements/word-of-mouth (verbal) together reach the whole locality fastest.
4iv. Public communicationpublic communication = one speaker addresses a large audience (assembly speech, seminar). Interpersonal = between 2 people; intrapersonal = with yourself; small group = team discussion.
5iv. Visual communicationposters, hoardings, banners and video ads attract the eye instantly and cross language barriers — ideal for advertising.
6ii. Senderthe sender encodes (converts thought into words/gestures); the receiver decodes (interprets it).
7ii. From receiver to senderfeedback is the receiver's response flowing BACK — it completes the loop.
8iii. Constructiveit points out the error AND gives a solution — that combination is constructive (the best type).
9ii. Linguisticdifferent languages / tough jargon = language barrier. Fix: simple words, visuals, translator.
10iii. Concreteconcrete = specific facts and figures (20%), not vague words like "a lot".
11ii. Topic sentenceparagraph order = topic sentence → supporting details → concluding line.
12iv. Interjectioninterjections express sudden feeling (Wow! Oh! Alas!) and end with "!"
13iii. "Please open page 10."imperative = order/request. (i) declarative, (ii) interrogative, (iv) exclamatory.
14ii. Writtenletters, emails and reports stay as proof and can be re-read; speech vanishes.
15iii. Visualsigns, signals, charts and symbols that you SEE = visual communication.
16i. Clearexact, simple, one-idea message with no confusion = clarity (vs vague "do it soon").
17iii. Culturaldifferent customs and gesture-meanings across cultures = cultural barrier.
18iii. Colourfulthe 7 Cs are Clear, Concise, Concrete, Correct, Coherent, Complete, Courteous — "Colourful" is the odd one.
19ii. Intrapersonalintra = within → communication WITH YOURSELF. Inter = between → with another person.
20iii. Organisationalrigid hierarchy/rules blocking message flow = organisational barrier. Fix: open-door policy, suggestion boxes.
21iii. Receiversender ENCODES (makes message), receiver DECODES (interprets it).
22iii. Smilea smile uses no words — it is non-verbal; the rest use words.
23ii. Tone, pitch and speed of voiceparalanguage = HOW you say it (tone/pitch/speed), a part of non-verbal communication.
24iii. Descriptiveit states only facts with no praise, blame or advice = descriptive.
25ii. Vague / generalit names no exact point. Specific version: "Your introduction was clear."
26ii. Delayedit arrives long after the act. Immediate feedback = instant, like a nod in class.
27ii. Immediate non-verbal feedbacka nod is instant (immediate) and uses no words (non-verbal).
28i. Conciseconcise = short, to the point, no extra words.
29ii. Completecomplete = all needed info (what, when, where, whom) included.
30iv. Courteouscourteous = polite and respectful language.
31iii. Coherentcoherent = ideas linked in logical order, no random jumps.
32iv. Correctcorrect = accurate facts, grammar and spellings.
